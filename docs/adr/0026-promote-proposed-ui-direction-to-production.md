# Promote the proposed UI direction to production

**Status:** accepted

The proposed direction from the mobile UI/UX prototype is now the production acceptance baseline for Donor, PIC, Admin, and SuperAdmin surfaces, including contextual mobile navigation. Contextual navigation uses role- and screen-specific app bars without introducing a new persistent bottom navigation system or changing the existing route model. Mobile is the canonical priority model; desktop preserves equivalent information and actions in wider layouts such as tables or grids. Admin and SuperAdmin share the action-first dashboard hierarchy while retaining their existing permission differences. Their shared queue includes member registrations first and supplemental donor approvals second; empty categories disappear and summaries/settings remain below operational work. Existing capabilities remain available and may only be removed through a separate product decision. When visual details conflict with clarity, localization, accessibility, real-data correctness, or capability preservation, those product qualities take precedence over literal prototype fidelity. The migration will be released in phases rather than as one coordinated cutover, with Donor and PIC surfaces first and Admin/SuperAdmin surfaces afterward. During Phase 1, the new and existing experiences coexist by role in the same deployment, with shared foundations introduced only when they do not unintentionally restyle legacy role surfaces. Phase 1 styling is scoped to Donor/PIC surfaces until the later Admin/SuperAdmin migration. Phase 1 covers authenticated Donor/PIC dashboard and campaign workflows across Open, Finalized, and Closed Campaign states; shared auth, onboarding, and profile screens remain functionally intact but are outside this visual migration. Existing combined-payment behavior remains grouped by destination bank account, with one proof submission and per-Campaign allocation and verification status visible. Outcomes are independent per Campaign even when they share one proof. After a Donor submits proof, that allocation remains read-only in the canonical Bukti Transfer perlu ditinjau state until the PIC accepts or rejects it; replacement upload begins only after rejection. Admin/SuperAdmin queue items open the relevant record for review before any approval or rejection confirmation; the notification affordance opens that same action queue rather than introducing a separate notification system. The PIC share affordance uses native device sharing when available and falls back to the existing copy-invitation capability. Existing bulk actions remain secondary, expose their affected count, require explicit confirmation, and are unavailable when the operation would be unsafe. Phase 1 must pass automated checks, real-data workflow verification, mobile and desktop checks, and explicit manual approval before Phase 2 begins. The prototype is frozen as a historical/reference artifact rather than maintained as a parallel implementation; production behavior and accepted ADRs become the source of truth. This expands and supersedes the deferral of contextual navigation and SuperAdmin prioritization in ADR 0024 so the implemented product matches the agreed role-first design.
